# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

Contributors must communicate respectfully, critique evidence rather than
people, disclose relevant conflicts of interest, and avoid discriminatory,
threatening, or abusive behavior. Maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable
contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Good
faith reports will be handled confidentially and without retaliation.

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, community spaces, and
public representation of the project.
